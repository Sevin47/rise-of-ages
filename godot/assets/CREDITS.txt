  pixx/     Free 2D Isometric Fantasy Pack
            Jana Ochse, 2D!PIXX, https://www.2dpixx.de
            https://store.godotengine.org/asset/twodpixx/test/

This one is not public domain. If any of these sprites ship in a build, that
credit line has to ship with it, somewhere a player can find it: an About
screen, a credits panel, or the store page. It is the only obligation the
licence imposes, and it is easy to forget once the art is just files in a
folder, which is why it is written down here next to them.

The characters are from this pack because Kenney has no isometric figures at
all. The catalogue has landscapes, buildings, roads and vehicles, but the
"Isometric Miniature Library" is a room full of bookcases, not people.
