Art in the Godot build.

All of it is generated by art/blender/render_buildings.py and
art/blender/render_world.py: buildings, ground tiles, trees, rocks and people.
Geometry is defined in Python, rendered through one orthographic camera under
one set of lights, and written straight into rendered/.

Nothing here is third-party, so nothing here carries an attribution
obligation. Earlier revisions used Kenney's isometric landscape tiles (CC0) and
2DPIXX's Free 2D Isometric Fantasy Pack (CC-BY 4.0, attribution required); both
are gone, and the CC-BY obligation with them. Those files are still in the git
history if they are ever wanted back.

The web build under /src and /public still uses Kenney art, which is CC0 and
asks for nothing.
