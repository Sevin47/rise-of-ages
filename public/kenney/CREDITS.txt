Art in this folder is by Kenney (https://kenney.nl) and is released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal), which places it in the public domain:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Credit is not required by the licence. It is given here because it is deserved.

Packs used
----------
tile/      Medieval RTS          https://kenney.nl/assets/medieval-rts
structure/ Medieval RTS          https://kenney.nl/assets/medieval-rts
unit/      Medieval RTS          https://kenney.nl/assets/medieval-rts
icon/      Board Game Icons      https://kenney.nl/assets/board-game-icons
audio/     Interface Sounds      https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds

Image files are the unmodified 64x64 "Default size" PNGs from those packs,
renamed to the short numeric names this project looks them up by. The mapping
from these files to what they represent lives in src/sprites.ts.

Audio files are unmodified .ogg clips from Interface Sounds, renamed for what
they do in the game (click, select, build, deny, raze, age, toggle).

The remaining artwork in this project — the wonder, trade-good and library-track
icons, and every panel texture — is original work and is not from Kenney. It
lives in src/art.ts.

The background music is not from Kenney either, and is not a recording at all:
it is generated at runtime in WebAudio by src/audio.ts. Kenney publishes jingles
but no looping score, and generating it avoided taking on a track under a
different licence.
